MWEA EAST JUNIOR SCHOOL TEACHERS’ WELFARE ORGANISATION (MEJUSTWO)CONSTITUTIONMwea East Sub-County, Kirinyaga County, Kenya
1Name

1.1 The name of the organisation shall be the Mwea East Junior School Teachers’ Welfare Organisation, abbreviated as MEJUSTWO (“the Welfare”).

2Purpose

2.1 The purpose of the Welfare is to bring together all Junior School teachers in Mwea East so as to cushion its members against financial constraints in times of need.

3Address and Area of Operation

3.1 The Welfare is founded by practising Junior School teachers, in both public and private schools, in Mwea East Sub-County only.

3.2 Mwea East Sub-County is in Kirinyaga County and has two zones, namely:

  1. Murinduko Zone; and

  2. Thiba Zone.

3.3 The Sub-County has approximately 50 Junior Schools, both public and private. The Sub-County headquarters is at Wang’uru, approximately 1½ km from the town.

4Nature and Goals of the Welfare

4.1 The Welfare is a non-political, non-religious, non-profit-making and non-racial welfare organisation.

4.2 It is a socio-economic welfare group and a regulated networking forum whose aim is to cushion its members against specified financial constraints, through the collective mobilisation of resources, so that members may assist one another in times of bereavement and in any other case that members may agree on from time to time.

5Specific Objectives

5.1 The Welfare shall undertake:

  1. Junior School teachers’ welfare matters; and

  2. any other activity that members may agree on.

6Core Values

6.1 Honesty and integrity – all members shall observe honesty in all matters of the Welfare.

6.2 Teamwork – all members shall collaborate with one another to achieve common goals and to complete tasks in the most efficient way.

6.3 Respect – all members shall be of good morals and shall treat one another with dignity.

6.4 Accountability – all Welfare finances shall be accounted for.

6.5 Transparency – every member shall be transparent in dealing with other members.

6.6 Equality – all members are equal and have equal opportunities to benefit from the Welfare.

7Activities of the Welfare

7.1 Matters Covered

The Welfare shall cater for the death of:

  1. the principal member;

  2. the principal member’s spouse (one);

  3. the principal member’s children, namely:

    1. biological children, irrespective of family set-up (whether nuclear, divorced, or a child brought into the marriage by the spouse), provided that the child’s birth certificate bears the name of the member as the parent or, in the case of a child brought into the marriage by the spouse, the name of the spouse;

    2. legally adopted children; and

    3. children up to the age of 22 years. This age limit shall not apply to children with evidence of permanent disability who are unable to support themselves; and

    the biological parents of the principal member only.

    7.2 Social Activities

    The Welfare may organise get-togethers, happy moments and road trips.

    7.3 Other Acts of Charity

    The Welfare may undertake any other act of charity that the Executive deems deserving on the basis of the needs of members. The Executive shall advise members on how to respond to such matters.

    7.4 Amounts to be Contributed

    1. On the death of a principal member, each member of the Welfare shall contribute KSh 1,000 (one thousand shillings).

    2. On the death of a member of the principal member’s nuclear family (the spouse or a child covered under clause 7.1), each member of the Welfare shall contribute KSh 500 (five hundred shillings).

    3. On the death of a biological parent of the principal member, each member of the Welfare shall contribute KSh 300 (three hundred shillings).

    4. Where a member’s hospital bill exceeds the limit covered by AON and NHIF (SHIF), members shall be mobilised to support the member through voluntary contributions.

    7.5 Reporting of Cases

    1. Where a case arises involving an eligible beneficiary under clause 7.1, the member shall inform the Executive, ordinarily through the Chairperson or any other executive member from the member’s zone (where applicable), within 48 hours. The Secretary shall then post the information on the Welfare’s official WhatsApp group (“the WhatsApp wall”). All members must be notified.

    2. A case reported after the burial shall not be considered by the Welfare. This promotes transparency, removes controversy and discourages fraudulent claims.

    7.6 Contributions

    There shall be no monthly contributions unless members agree otherwise. Contributions shall be made only when a case covered by the Welfare arises.

    7.7 Deadlines

    The deadline for contributions shall be three (3) days before the date on which the event is scheduled to take place. The events include bereavement cases and the AGM or SGM.

    7.8 Logistics and Operations Kitty

    To ensure that all members receive full benefits, there shall be an operations kitty to cater for the transport expenses of members visiting bereaved families and for other logistics. These expenses shall be met by the participating members where applicable, or sourced from registration fees or any other appropriate means convenient to members.

    An account of the operations kitty shall be given at the end of every case and at AGMs and SGMs.

8Membership

8.1 Qualifications for Membership

The Welfare is open to all practising Junior School teachers in Mwea East only, whether in private or public schools, who meet its terms and conditions.

8.2 Late Registration

  1. A person who fails to join the Welfare within the stipulated time frame during its formation shall register upon payment of KSh 500. That person shall serve a probation period of three (3) months or three (3) consecutive cases, whichever comes first, from the date the fee is paid in full, and shall not benefit from the Welfare during that period. This prevents individuals from taking advantage of other members.

  2. Registration fees paid under this clause shall be applied to the miscellaneous kitty in accordance with Article 12.

8.3 Loss of Membership

A member shall lose membership if:

  1. the member dies;

  2. the member defaults on any two cases and, after being summoned by the Executive to explain himself or herself, a final decision is made and communicated to members. This helps to maintain discipline and to ensure that deadlines are honoured; or

  3. the member engages in fraud in accordance with clause 17.2.

A member who loses membership shall be struck off the membership list and the WhatsApp wall. A member who wishes to rejoin shall pay all amounts that other members have contributed since the membership was lost, shall serve a probation period of three (3) months, and shall forgo Welfare benefits for three (3) months from the date the arrears are paid in full.

8.4 Succession

Where a member dies, the member’s eligible spouse may replace the deceased member, whether or not the spouse is a teacher, or is a teacher outside Mwea East.

8.5 Teachers Not on the WhatsApp Wall

Teachers who are not on the WhatsApp wall but whose contributions are up to date qualify as bona fide members.

8.6 Voluntary Withdrawal

A member may exit the Welfare of his or her own will by official notification to the Executive through the Zonal Representatives.

8.7 Discipline of a Member

  1. A member who fails to remit his or her contribution within the stipulated deadline shall be liable to a fine of 50% of the principal amount being contributed.

  2. A member who rejoins after losing membership shall comply with the conditions set out in clause 8.3.

  3. A member who abuses another through insults, uses derogatory or obscene language, posts obscene material, or fails to maintain order shall be suspended from the WhatsApp group for seven (7) calendar days. A member who is suspended twice in a year shall be removed from the WhatsApp group for one year and shall write an apology letter when being admitted back.

8.8 Rights of a Member

A member has the right:

  1. to propose constitutional amendments through the procedures outlined in this Constitution;

  2. to demand accountability for the Welfare’s finances following the stipulated procedure; and

  3. to be treated with respect.

8.9 Duties of a Member

A member shall:

  1. attend all meetings on time;

  2. participate in decision-making through casting votes and regulated debates;

  3. make contributions and any other payments as outlined in this Constitution;

  4. share any information relevant to the Welfare through the Chairperson;

  5. observe this Constitution at all times; and

  6. respect the officials.

9Office Bearers and Governance

9.1 The Executive

The Executive shall consist of six (6) members:

  1. the Chairperson;

  2. the Vice Chairperson;

  3. the Treasurer;

  4. the Secretary;

  5. the Vice Secretary; and

  6. the Organising Secretary.

9.2 The Governing Body

The Governing Body is the highest authority of the Welfare. It shall comprise the Executive, the Zonal Representatives and a representative of persons with special needs.

9.3 Gender Balance

The Executive must have gender balance. It shall not comprise more than two-thirds (2/3) of the same gender.

9.4 Voting in the Governing Body

A two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the whole Governing Body shall be required for any matter under discussion to be carried.

9.5 Representation

The Governing Body must reflect the face of Mwea East. Each zone (where applicable) shall have a Representative, who shall represent the Welfare at zonal level, and a representative of persons with special needs.

9.6 Eligibility to Vote and to Vie

All members are eligible to vote and to vie for office, except members on probation.

9.7 Responsibilities and Duties of Officials

Chairperson

  1. Is the head of the Welfare and shall convene and preside over its meetings.

  2. Shall be the spokesperson of the Welfare.

  3. Shall attend to all administrative matters concerning the Welfare.

  4. Shall inform members of any cases received from the Zonal Representatives.

  5. Shall coordinate and ensure the smooth running of all Welfare activities.

  6. Shall ensure that the function of each office holder is performed as outlined in this Constitution.

  7. Shall determine contentious matters that are to be put to a vote, in consultation with other members of the Executive where applicable.

  8. Shall perform any other duty outlined in this Constitution.

Vice Chairperson

The Vice Chairperson shall perform all the duties of the Chairperson in his or her absence.

Secretary

  1. Shall be the secretary to the Governing Body and to all executive meetings.

  2. Shall be in charge of Welfare documentation, including writing and keeping the minutes of meetings.

  3. Shall maintain the records of members.

  4. Shall be in charge of correspondence, in consultation with the Chairperson.

  5. Shall perform any other duty assigned by the Governing Body or the Chairperson.

  6. Shall, with the authority of the Chairperson, issue notices of general meetings fourteen (14) days before the scheduled meeting.

Vice Secretary

In the absence of the Secretary, the Vice Secretary shall perform all the duties of the Secretary, and such other duties as the Secretary or the Governing Body may assign.

Treasurer

  1. Is the Welfare’s chief financial and accounting officer.

  2. Shall receive, and shall disburse under the direction of the Executive, all money collected.

  3. Shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, a financial statement and ensure accountability and transparency in respect of all money collected. The statement shall include, but not be limited to:

    1. cases handled and amounts disbursed;

    2. amounts injected into the miscellaneous kitty through registration, donations, fines or any other source; and

    3. expenditure from the miscellaneous kitty.

    Shall keep true records, past and present, of all monies collected and disbursed for and on behalf of the Welfare.

    Shall remind members of their contributions to avoid late payment and penalties. This may be done through Zonal Representatives, or by SMS, WhatsApp or any other means the Treasurer deems appropriate.

    Organising Secretary

    The Organising Secretary shall perform such duties as the Chairperson or the Governing Body may specifically assign and, in the absence of both the Secretary and the Vice Secretary, shall perform the duties of the Secretary.

    Zonal Representatives (Point Persons)

    1. Shall be the contact person between the Executive and the members of his or her zone.

    2. Shall receive cases from members and report them to the Chairperson.

    3. Shall be an ambassador of the Welfare and spearhead its interests in his or her zone.

    4. Shall induct new members in his or her zone and give them full information about the operations and benefits of the Welfare.

    5. Shall avail this Constitution to new members in his or her zone.

    9.8 Disqualification and Removal from Office

    Zonal Representatives

    A Zonal Representative shall cease to hold office if he or she:

    1. resigns or defaults; or

    2. is removed for gross misconduct.

    Where a Zonal Representative is accused of gross misconduct, the Executive shall summon the official to explain himself or herself, and an impeachment procedure shall be undertaken in which two-thirds (2/3) of the whole Governing Body must vote in favour of removal.

    Executive Members

    An executive member shall lose office on any of the following grounds:

    1. resignation;

    2. deregistration;

    3. engagement in fraud; or

    4. impeachment for gross misconduct.

    An executive member may be impeached for gross misconduct by a majority of at least two-thirds (2/3) of the total membership, through voting.

    Any member may initiate an impeachment motion with the support of one-third (1/3) of the total membership, who must show their support on an organised platform on the WhatsApp wall for the motion to be put to a vote by all members.

    Any impeachment of an executive member shall be made through the Chairperson or, in the Chairperson’s absence, the Vice Chairperson.

    Transfer of an Executive Member

    1. An executive member shall lose his or her position upon transfer to another sub-county.

    2. The vacancy shall be filled through an election conducted in accordance with Article 16, with voting held within seven (7) days.

10Tenure of Office

10.1 An executive member shall serve for a period of three (3) years, renewable.

10.2 A Zonal Representative shall serve for a period of three (3) years, renewable, and shall be replaced if he or she:

  1. resigns;

  2. transfers to another sub-county or county;

  3. is involved in fraud;

  4. is deregistered; or

  5. is removed for gross misconduct.

11Welfare Finances

11.1 Sources of Funds

  1. Registration fees from members;

  2. members’ contributions to finance a case; and

  3. fines collected from defaulters.

11.2 Financial Reporting

The Treasurer shall account for all funds and give a financial report at the AGM or SGM.

11.3 Welfare Account

  1. The Welfare shall have a bank account.

  2. All donations and surpluses of case contributions shall be paid or deposited into this account.

  3. All registration fees for members joining the Welfare, and all fines, shall be paid directly into this account, either through paybill or by direct deposit.

  4. The account shall be used for any other Welfare purposes as the Executive may deem right.

11.4 Bank Signatories

All executive members shall be bank signatories. The Chairperson and any two other signatories shall sign for any transaction to proceed. This prevents the misuse of Welfare monies and promotes transparency in expenditure.

12Utilisation of Funds

12.1 Contributions accumulated from new member registrations, donations, fines and surpluses of case contributions shall form a miscellaneous kitty, which shall be used to cater for collective Welfare expenses such as:

  1. subsidising cases;

  2. subsidising the AGM or SGM;

  3. registering and renewing the Welfare’s registration certificate;

  4. voting expenses, such as airtime and data bundles; and

  5. executive meetings (snacks and soft drinks).

12.2 All funds collected from members during a case shall be channelled to the bona fide beneficiary member of that case, using the procedure laid down in Article 7.

13Welfare Assets

13.1 The Welfare shall keep an inventory of all its assets, clearly indicating who has custody of each asset.

13.2 Any amount donated to the Welfare during an AGM or SGM shall be discussed in the Welfare’s meetings with regard to its distribution among the members present.

14Record Management

14.1 The following records shall be maintained:

  1. Financial file – for financial statements and receipts, kept by the Treasurer;

  2. Procurement file – for all documents used in purchasing items for the Welfare;

  3. Minutes file – containing the proposed and seconded minutes;

  4. Members’ register – containing each member’s name, contact, sub-county, county and station of work, together with a list of all cases handled and all deregistered members;

  5. General report file – for the general report to be prepared every two years and handed to the Social Development Officer;

  6. Annual finance report file – for the annual finance report to be handed to the Social Development Office; and

  7. Election file – containing screenshots of all members’ voting ballots.

14.2 All members shall have the right to access any record on request.

15Meetings

15.1 Types of Meetings

The following meetings shall be held:

  1. Executive meetings – the Executive shall meet whenever there is a need;

  2. Virtual meetings – which may be held as the need arises;

  3. Governing Body meetings – the Governing Body shall meet whenever there is a need;

  4. Annual General Meeting (AGM) – held every calendar year in the month of July; and

  5. Special General Meeting (SGM) – which may be held where the need arises.

15.2 Quorum

  1. The quorum for an AGM or SGM shall not be less than one-third (1/3) of the registered members.

  2. The quorum for an executive meeting shall be half of the total number of the Executive.

  3. The quorum for a Governing Body meeting shall be half of the total number of the Governing Body.

15.3 Notice of Meetings

Before any meeting, the Secretary shall give notice of at least two weeks (fourteen days) in writing, through electronic media, SMS, WhatsApp or any other means the Secretary deems appropriate, and shall give the agenda of the meeting.

15.4 Decision-Making

  1. A simple majority of the relevant quorum shall pass any matter that is within the Constitution.

  2. All major decisions of the Welfare shall be made during the AGM.

  3. In the event of an equality of votes, the Chairperson shall have a casting vote.

  4. Where a resolution requires a constitutional amendment, it shall be subjected to a referendum in accordance with the procedure laid down in Article 18.

15.5 Agenda of the AGM and SGM

  1. Reading and confirmation of the previous minutes.

  2. Matters arising from the previous minutes.

  3. The Treasurer’s report.

  4. The Chairperson’s report, including the challenges and achievements of the Welfare.

  5. Such other matters as the Executive may deem fit.

  6. Any other business, with the approval of the Chairperson.

15.6 Funding of the AGM and SGM

  1. All members shall contribute towards the AGM and SGM.

  2. The Executive shall prepare a budget and, in consultation with all members through the Zonal Representatives, shall decide the amount to be contributed by each member, according to the budget and the prevailing economic situation.

  3. The AGM and SGM shall be treated as a case in which each member is required to pay the meeting expenses.

16Elections

16.1 Eligibility

Eligibility to vote and to vie is as provided in clause 9.6.

16.2 Supervision of Elections

The returning officials shall be the current members of the Executive.

16.3 Method of Voting

  1. Voting shall be conducted through the open WhatsApp online platform, verifiable by every member. If the platform has technological challenges, any other means that the Executive may prefer, which caters for the availability of all members and provides a convenient avenue to vote, shall be adopted.

  2. Members who cannot access the WhatsApp wall shall send their vote to the Chairperson by text message. The Chairperson (or the Vice Chairperson in the Chairperson’s absence) shall screenshot the vote and post it on the WhatsApp wall as an image. Such a vote shall be valid.

16.4 Notice of Elections

Members shall be given proper notice of the time and venue of voting through text messages, WhatsApp and the Zonal Representatives.

16.5 Timing and Handover

Elections shall be held one month before the fourth-year AGM, at which the handover shall take place and those elected shall assume office. The outgoing officials shall have no power to initiate constitutional amendments.

16.6 Integrity of Votes

No member shall delete a vote or vote twice. Where a vote is deleted, the original vote shall be the one that counts. Any such act shall be treated as fraud and the member shall be expelled from the Welfare.

16.7 Candidacy and Winning Threshold

Those interested in vying for office shall send their requests to the Chairperson (or the Vice Chairperson in the Chairperson’s absence) for consideration. A candidate must gather two-thirds (2/3) of the votes cast.

16.8 Run-off Voting

Where there are many candidates and none obtains a two-thirds (2/3) majority in the first round, voting shall be repeated, eliminating the candidate with the fewest votes in each round, until only two candidates remain. The candidate with the simple majority of votes between the two shall be declared the winner.

16.9 Zonal Representatives

A zone must have at least thirty (30) active members to be accorded a Representative.

Where a zone has no members, or its active membership falls below fifteen (15), or its sitting Representative resigns or transfers, the Executive shall, within fourteen (14) days from the date the situation arises, either call an election, merge the zone with the other zone under one Representative, or declare the zone’s representation null and void.

17Dispute Resolution and Fraud

17.1 Dispute Resolution

  1. To avoid disputes, the Executive shall take responsibility for maintaining peace among members during meetings and on the WhatsApp wall.

  2. The Executive shall be the administrators of the Welfare’s WhatsApp group and shall moderate and monitor discussions and maintain order.

  3. Only the Chairperson shall be authorised to add or remove a member, in consultation with the Executive.

  4. If the Executive is unable to resolve an issue, the Welfare shall consult the relevant government official or the Social Development Officer for guidance.

  5. Any person who campaigns or posts any information other than the vote during voting time shall be suspended from the Welfare for seven (7) days. This helps to maintain order.

  6. Abuse of another member, including insults, derogatory terms, obscene material or failure to maintain order, shall be dealt with in accordance with clause 8.7(c).

17.2 Fraud

  1. Any member who engages in or facilitates the defrauding of Welfare members through giving false information shall be expelled from the Welfare permanently, and legal proceedings shall be initiated in accordance with the law under which the Welfare is registered.

  2. To deal with this vice, a member may be required to produce the following documents, in original form, where necessary:

    1. a copy of the eulogy (funeral programme) clearly referring to the deceased as wife, husband, father, mother, son or daughter;

    2. a copy of the birth certificate of the child, a birth notification or legal adoption papers;

    3. the identity card of the spouse or a death certificate (where the spouse was already married when he or she had a child);

    4. the identity card of the departed parent, the birth certificate of the member, and burial permits; and

    5. any other document that the Executive may declare necessary.

18Amendment of the Constitution

18.1 Voting Threshold and Method

Any change to this Constitution may be made by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the total membership, by a vote conducted through the WhatsApp online platform or during the AGM. If the platform has technological challenges, any other means that the Executive may prefer, which caters for the availability of all members and provides a convenient avenue to vote, shall be adopted.

Members whose phones cannot access WhatsApp shall send their vote to the Chairperson by text message. The Chairperson (or the Vice Chairperson in the Chairperson’s absence) shall screenshot the vote and post it on the WhatsApp wall as an image. Such a vote shall be valid.

18.2 Procedure for Proposing an Amendment

  1. A member may initiate a constitutional change with the support of one-third (1/3) of the total number of members, for the amendment to be put to a vote by all members. The supporting third must show their interest on an official platform on the Welfare’s WhatsApp wall.

  2. A Zonal Representative (Point Person) may initiate a constitutional change with the support of one-third (1/3) of the total number of Point Persons. The amendment shall be put to a vote of the Governing Body, in which two-thirds (2/3) of the total number of Governing Body members must vote in favour before it is put to a vote by all members. The supporting third must show their interest on an official platform on the Welfare’s WhatsApp wall.

  3. The Executive may propose constitutional amendments through the Chairperson.

18.3 Voting

Voting shall be conducted during the AGM and shall be open for thirty (30) minutes. Members must be given proper notice of the time and date of voting through the Zonal Representatives, WhatsApp and text messages.

18.4 Adoption of Amendments

Results shall be announced and adopted within twenty-four (24) hours after the voting exercise.

18.5 Returning Officials

The returning officials shall be the current members of the Executive.

18.6 Channel for Amendments and Impeachment

Any constitutional amendment or impeachment of an executive officer shall be made through the Chairperson.

18.7 Notification of the Social Development Officer

Any amendment must be brought to the attention of the Social Development Officer.

19Indemnity

19.1 The Executive shall not pay compensation for any loss, damage or injury caused to third parties by activities of the Welfare undertaken with the authority of the Welfare through minutes.

20Dissolution

20.1 The Welfare shall be dissolved:

  1. if two-thirds (2/3) of the members agree to dissolve the Welfare and obtain a dissolution form from the Social Development Office;

  2. if the government decides to dissolve the Welfare; or

  3. if the Welfare’s registration certificate is not renewed for three (3) years.

20.2 Before dissolution, the Welfare shall:

  1. pay all liabilities;

  2. dispose of all its assets; and

  3. divide all money in the account among its members.

20.3 Where any of the circumstances in clause 20.1 occurs, the following procedure shall be followed:

  1. signing of the dissolution form;

  2. closing of the account; and

  3. returning of the Welfare’s registration certificate to the Social Development Office.

21Commitment Clause

21.1 Each member joining the Welfare shall be given a copy of this Constitution, allowed to read it, and required to sign to commit to its provisions by paying the registration fee. A non-transferable registration number shall be issued to the member.
